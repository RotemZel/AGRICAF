Pairwise Predictor Interactions
Friedman’s H, hierarchical edge bundling
Reading the figure

Each node is a predictor, coloured by type, with the four types labelled around the perimeter.

An edge carries Friedman’s H for that pair: the share of the pair’s joint effect on the forecast that cannot be reproduced by adding their two separate effects. H runs from 0, where the two predictors act additively, to 1, where their effect is entirely joint.

Edge weight and opacity scale with H. Note that H is normalised by the pair’s own joint effect, so a high value identifies a relationship that is largely interactive, not a pair that is influential in absolute terms.

Values are averaged over the explained years. Only predictors holding at least 2% of the case’s permutation importance are paired against the rest.

Hover a label to isolate its edges and list its partners, strongest first.
